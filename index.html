Frequently asked questions

Does this replace my employees?

No. The receptionist is designed to help answer incoming calls and capture information so your team can focus on the work that needs them.

Can it answer questions about my roofing company?

Yes. We configure it with the business details and approved answers you provide, such as services, hours, and service area.

Can I review it before it goes live?

Yes. We test your customized call flow with you and make adjustments before activation.

Do I need to change my business phone number?

Usually, call forwarding can let you keep your existing business number. We'll discuss the setup that fits your phone provider.

What happens if a caller needs a person?

We can configure the call flow to capture a callback request or route calls according to the instructions and phone setup agreed with you.
