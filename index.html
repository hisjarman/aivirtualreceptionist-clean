THE DIFFERENCE IS THE SERVICE

You shouldn't have to become an AI engineer to answer your phone better.

Think about what happens when a customer calls your business. They may be comparing providers. They may have water coming through a ceiling, an air conditioner that stopped working, a broken pipe, a question about an appointment or a project they want quoted. They are not necessarily going to call three times and leave a voicemail. Often, they move on to the next company.

And when you're busy, answering every call personally isn't realistic. You might be driving, meeting a customer, working with equipment, managing a crew or already speaking to someone who needs your attention. Hiring a full-time receptionist can be expensive, and even a human receptionist has working hours, breaks and competing tasks.

That's why we built a managed alternative.

We don't hand you a login and wish you luck. We start by understanding how your business works. We learn what services you offer, what areas you cover, what information matters, what questions callers ask and what should happen when a caller needs help. Then we configure an AI receptionist around those rules.

We create the call experience, test it, and make refinements with you. We help connect the agreed phone routing. After launch, we remain your service contact for the configuration and support included in your plan. Your team gets a consistent first response without having to build and maintain the underlying AI workflow themselves.

What white-glove means here.

Personalized onboarding: we gather the details that make your receptionist sound and behave like it belongs to your business.

Managed configuration: your agent's call instructions, voice settings, business knowledge and workflow are configured for your needs.

Practical testing: we test typical calls and edge cases before launch, and you have a chance to request changes.

Ongoing assistance: when your hours, services, policies or routing change, you have a clear way to request updates under your plan.

Clear usage: packages include a stated pool of AI call minutes. We explain what counts as usage, what happens when you approach your allowance and what any additional usage costs before you commit.

It is a service relationship, not a software scavenger hunt. You tell us what a good call should look like. We take responsibility for building the agreed experience and helping you keep it useful.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START?

Know what you're getting.

Clear answers, straightforward onboarding and no surprise setup work.

What exactly is included in the monthly minutes?

The package includes the stated number of AI receptionist call minutes in each monthly billing period. Provider billing can include connected conversation time, including listening or hold time while the AI agent remains active. We will explain the final usage definition and your specific telephony arrangement before activation.

Do unused minutes roll over?

Yes. Unused included minutes carry into the immediately following billing month only. The rollover balance is capped at one full monthly plan allowance (300, 1,000 or 2,500 minutes). Rollover minutes are used before the new monthly allowance and expire at the end of that following month if unused. Minutes do not accumulate beyond one month, have no cash value and are not refundable. Rollover is available only while your subscription is active and paid. The same rule applies to monthly and annual plans.

What happens if I use all my minutes?

We will disclose the overage rate for your selected configuration before checkout. You can also request a plan upgrade. We will not describe the service as unlimited.

Why can Retell's per-minute cost change?

Retell's pricing depends on the voice engine, language model, telephony route and optional features. Different countries and phone providers can have different rates. We base your final quote on the configuration we plan to deploy.

Do I have to replace my existing phone number?

Often, you can keep your current business number and forward calls to the AI receptionist, depending on your carrier and call-routing setup. We review this during onboarding.

Is every customer given a separate receptionist?

Our managed architecture is designed around separate customer configurations so each business has its own call instructions, business information, routing and agent setup. Your business details are not simply swapped into a shared generic script.

Can it book appointments or transfer calls?

Those workflows may be configured when supported by your calendar, phone system and selected integrations. We confirm the exact workflow during discovery and do not promise unsupported integrations.

Can I hear a demo before buying?

Yes. Submit the discovery form and choose a personalized demo. We will use the details you provide to prepare a demonstration based on your industry and call scenarios.

What is the setup fee?

Monthly subscribers pay a one-time onboarding and configuration fee. The amount depends on the complexity of your call flow and is quoted before payment. Customers who prepay annually have that setup fee waived.

Can I pay with PayPal?

PayPal checkout is planned. Live PayPal subscription checkout requires the business PayPal account's client ID and the monthly and annual billing plan IDs, plus a secure backend or approved PayPal subscription integration. Until that is connected and tested, this form collects your plan selection and billing preference but does not charge your card or PayPal account.
