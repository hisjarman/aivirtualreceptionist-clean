WHY THE PREMIUM?

You're paying for someone to make the technology work for your business.

The monthly difference is not a mystery fee. It is the difference between renting a tool and hiring a service provider to configure and help manage that tool.

1. We learn how your business actually operates.

A generic receptionist may know your company name and opening hours. A useful business receptionist needs more: your service area, services, common questions, qualification rules, urgent-call procedure, handoff preferences and the details your team needs to follow up. We collect this information and turn it into a practical call experience.

2. We configure a separate experience for your business.

Your callers should not hear another company's greeting or receive answers based on another company's policies. Our managed delivery model is designed around a separate client configuration, with its own business knowledge, call instructions, routing and agent setup.

3. We test the calls before you rely on them.

We work through normal and unusual scenarios: a new lead, a returning customer, an after-hours caller, a caller outside your service area, an urgent issue and someone who asks for a person. We refine the agreed flow before launch. No AI system is perfect, so testing and clear escalation rules matter.

4. You have a service contact when the business changes.

Businesses change their hours, service areas, staff, promotions and procedures. With a self-service product, you may be responsible for finding the setting, editing the prompt and checking whether the change worked. With our managed plan, you have an agreed way to request configuration assistance and updates.

5. We help you avoid the hidden work.

The software subscription is only one part of the job. Someone still needs to design the conversation, organize business information, configure the phone flow, test it, monitor usage and troubleshoot issues. If you or an employee do that work, it still costs time. Our premium is for taking on the agreed setup and management work.

6. You can focus on the customer in front of you.

If you are on a job site, driving, treating a patient, meeting a client or speaking with another customer, you should not have to stop everything to configure software. The point is to make your phone operation more dependable while you focus on delivering the service you sell.

Honest distinction: Lower-cost competitors may be the right choice if you enjoy configuring software, have simple call needs or want the lowest possible monthly cost. Our service is designed for owners who would rather have someone set up and help manage the receptionist. We do not claim that every competitor lacks customization or support; the exact scope differs by plan and should be checked before purchase.
A SIMPLE WAY TO THINK ABOUT IT

DIY software vs. managed service

Both can use AI to answer calls. The difference is who owns the setup and day-to-day configuration work.

🧰

Self-service platform

You choose the plan, build or customize the agent, enter business information, connect your phone workflow and maintain the configuration. Best if you want hands-on control and the lowest platform price.

🤝

Our managed service

We guide discovery, configure the agreed experience, test it with you and provide ongoing assistance for changes covered by your plan. Best if you value time and expert help over doing the setup yourself.

👤

Human answering service

A person answers calls. This may suit sensitive conversations or callers who specifically need human interaction, but it is typically priced differently and can cost substantially more than AI-only service.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions business owners ask

Do unused minutes roll over?

Yes. Unused minutes carry into the next billing month only, capped at one monthly plan allowance. Rollover minutes are consumed first and expire at the end of that next month if unused. They do not stack month after month, have no cash value and are not refundable. Your subscription must remain active and paid. This applies to both monthly and annual billing.

Why are you more expensive than Rosie or Dialzara?

Those services publish lower-cost AI receptionist plans. Our pricing includes a managed setup and support relationship: business discovery, separate configuration, tailored call flow, testing and agreed ongoing assistance. If you prefer to configure and maintain an AI receptionist yourself, a lower-cost platform may be a better fit.

Does the monthly price include unlimited changes?

No. It includes managed assistance within the scope of your selected plan. The exact support scope, response expectations, complex workflow changes and any additional fees are confirmed before purchase.

Are all features available in every package?

Core call answering and business-specific configuration are the foundation. Booking, CRM connections, transfers, SMS and other integrations depend on the required third-party systems and technical compatibility. We confirm what is included in your proposal; we do not imply that unsupported integrations are automatic.

Are your prices directly comparable with the providers listed?

Not exactly. Some providers bill by unique caller or call rather than minutes, and they package onboarding and support differently. The comparison is intended to help you understand the market, not to claim identical usage or feature sets.

What if I only want the cheapest AI answering tool?

Then a self-service provider may be the better choice. Our service is for owners who want personalized configuration and someone to help manage the experience.
