GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Frequently asked questions.

A few things business owners usually want to know before getting started.

Will I have to change my business phone number?

In many cases, no. Call forwarding may allow you to keep your current number. We'll review your phone provider and recommend a suitable routing setup.

Can the receptionist answer questions about my company?

Yes. We configure it with the business information and approved answers you provide, such as your services, hours, service area and common questions.

Can callers speak to a real person?

We can configure transfer or callback options based on your preferences and the capabilities of your phone setup.

Can I test it before customers call?

Yes. We'll test example calls with you and make adjustments before you approve the receptionist for launch.

Does it guarantee more sales?

No service can guarantee a particular number of leads or sales. The goal is to provide a consistent first response and capture information that might otherwise be missed.

What happens after I request a demo?

We'll review your business details, discuss how you want calls handled and arrange the next step for a personalized demonstration.
